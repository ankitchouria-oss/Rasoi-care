Common questions

Do you carry spare parts to the first visit?

Technicians stock the 40 fastest-moving parts for your appliance brand. Anything rarer is ordered on the spot and fitted within 48 hours at no second visit charge.

What if the problem returns?

Every repair carries a 90-day warranty on labour and fitted parts. Reopen the job from your booking history and the same technician is assigned first.

How is the price decided?

You pay a fixed visit and diagnosis fee. Parts are quoted in the app before any work starts — you approve each line before the technician proceeds.

Can I reschedule?

Free until 2 hours before the slot. After that a ₹99 fee applies because the technician is already routed to you.
